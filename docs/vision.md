# Vision

## Purpose

The purpose of this application is to help school leaders, student health teams, teachers, and instructional leaders make better decisions through data-informed insights.

The application must transform complex school data into clear, actionable information that supports everyday decision-making at all levels of the organization.

## Core Mission

The ultimate goal is simple:

> Every student should have the best possible opportunity to succeed, develop, and reach their full potential.

All features, visualizations, analyses, recommendations, and workflows must contribute to this mission.

## What Success Looks Like

### For School Leaders

A school leader can quickly understand:

- How students are performing academically.
- Which students, classes, or year groups may need additional support.
- Trends in attendance, achievement, wellbeing, and progression.
- How resources and staffing align with student needs.
- Whether interventions are producing measurable results.

### For Teachers and Instructional Leaders

A teacher can quickly understand:

- Which students are progressing as expected.
- Which students need support or challenge.
- Patterns in learning, attendance, and assessment outcomes.
- The impact of teaching strategies and interventions.

### For Student Health Teams

A student health team can quickly understand:

- Which students may be at risk.
- Early warning indicators based on attendance, achievement, and wellbeing data.
- Where proactive support can have the greatest impact.

## Guiding Principles

### Student-Centric

Every metric, dashboard, and analysis must ultimately relate back to improving outcomes for students.

### Actionable Insights

The application should not merely display data. It should help users understand:

- What is happening.
- Why it may be happening.
- What actions could be considered next.

### Early Identification

The system should help identify risks and opportunities as early as possible so support can be provided before problems become significant.

### Simplicity

Complex educational data should be presented in a way that is easy to understand for non-technical users.

### Trust

All insights must be transparent, explainable, and based on clearly visible underlying data.

## Demo Environment

This application is a demonstration platform using synthetic school data.

The demo school consists of:

- Year 1–10
- Two classes per year level
- Twenty students per class
- Synthetic attendance, assessment, wellbeing, staffing, and financial data

The purpose of the demo is to showcase how a future data-informed primary school in Gothenburg could operate.

## Product North Star

When making design or development decisions, always prioritize the option that most effectively helps educators understand their school and improve outcomes for students.

## LLM Development Directive

When iterating on this product, always evaluate features, designs, dashboards, workflows, and recommendations against the following question:

> Does this help educators make better decisions that improve student outcomes?

If the answer is no, the feature should be redesigned, simplified, or removed.

The success of the application is **not** measured by the amount of data displayed.

The success of the application is measured by:

1. Better understanding of student needs.
2. Earlier identification of risks and opportunities.
3. More effective allocation of resources and support.
4. Better educational outcomes.
5. More students reaching their full potential.

This directive takes precedence over technical complexity, visual sophistication, or feature quantity.
